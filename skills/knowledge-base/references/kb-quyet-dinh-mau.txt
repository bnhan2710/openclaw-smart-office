QUYET DINH BAN HANH QUY TRINH CAP NHAT KNOWLEDGE BASE

I. QUYET DINH
1. Ban hanh quy trinh cap nhat knowledge base noi bo.
2. Quy dinh index phai co schema_version va updated_at.

II. PHAM VI AP DUNG
1. Ap dung voi tat ca tai lieu trong kho knowledge base.
2. Ap dung voi nhan su Van thu, CNTT va nghiep vu.

III. TRACH NHIEM THUC HIEN
1. Phong CNTT: quan ly index, migration, benchmark.
2. Van thu: bo sung tai lieu nguon va metadata chinh xac.
3. Bo phan Nghiep vu: xac nhan ket qua truy hoi.

IV. HIEU LUC
1. Quyet dinh co hieu luc ke tu ngay ky.
2. Van ban nay thay the cac huong dan truoc do ve KB neu co mâu thuan.
