QUY CHE NOI BO - TIEP NHAN VA XU LY CONG VAN (BAN KHUNG)

I. PHAM VI VA DINH NGHIA
1. Van ban nay ap dung cho tat ca cong van den tai Don vi.
2. Cong van khan (CVK): cong van co dau KHAN/HOA TOC.
3. Cong van thuong: cong van khong co dau KHAN/HOA TOC.

II. TIEP NHAN CONG VAN
1. Cong van den duoc tiep nhan tai Van thu trong gio hanh chinh.
2. Ngoai gio hanh chinh, Van thu ghi nhan va bao cao lanh dao truc.
3. Ban scan (PDF) phai duoc luu tru tren he thong luu tru noi bo.

III. PHAN LOAI VA XU LY
1. Cong van KHAN/HOA TOC: trinh lanh dao trong 2 gio ke tu khi tiep nhan.
2. Cong van thuong: chuyen phong/bo phan lien quan trong 24 gio.
3. Neu cong van lien quan den nhieu don vi, Van thu ghi ro don vi chu tri va phoi hop.

IV. THOI HAN TRA LOI
1. Cong van KHAN/HOA TOC: phai co du thao tra loi trong 1 ngay lam viec.
2. Cong van thuong: du thao tra loi trong 5 ngay lam viec.
3. Truong hop phuc tap co the xin gia han, nhung khong qua 10 ngay.

V. VAO SO VA LUU TRU
1. Tat ca cong van den phai vao so va luu tru.
2. Thoi han luu tru toi thieu 5 nam.
3. Ban scan luu tai thu muc: /LuuTru/CongVanDen/YYYY/MM/.

VI. PHAN CONG NHIEM VU (TOM TAT)
1. Giao Phong Hanh chinh cap nhat so cong van hang ngay.
2. Giao Phong CNTT duy tri he thong luu tru va sao luu dinh ky vao thu 6 hang tuan.
3. De nghi Van thu thong bao danh sach cong van khan cho lanh dao trong 2 gio.

VII. CAN CU VA HIEU LUC
1. Can cu: Nghi dinh 30/2020/ND-CP ve cong tac van thu.
2. Van ban co hieu luc ke tu 01/05/2026.

PHU LUC A - MAU BIEU
1. Mau so 01: Phieu tiep nhan cong van.
2. Mau so 02: Phieu chuyen xu ly cong van.
